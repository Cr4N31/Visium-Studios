Executive summary

The most meaningful gains are the new Insights experience, dedicated capability detail pages, a more structured Studio story and foundational SEO improvements. Portfolio, navigation and animation refinements bring the new and existing journeys into closer alignment.

01 / CONTENT

Insights, from archive to article

Added a browsable, category-filtered Insights archive and individual article pages, backed by structured content and direct routes. Studio Notes now points into the editorial experience.

02 / SERVICES

Deeper capability journeys

Added dedicated capability detail pages and refined the service grid, giving visitors a clearer path from the overview to individual offerings.

03 / STUDIO

A more considered studio story

Reworked the Studio page around distinct environment, process and work sections, alongside refreshed team data and profile content.

04 / DISCOVERY

Search and sharing foundations

Added route-aware SEO titles and descriptions, canonical and Open Graph metadata, plus sitemap and robots files. Hosting rewrites preserve those files for crawlers.

05 / WORK & ENQUIRIES

Clearer portfolio-to-contact paths

Refined project cards, the work grid and case-study layouts; improved internal navigation and added a reusable floating project CTA across relevant pages.

06 / EXPERIENCE

More consistent interaction and polish

Consolidated motion and pointer behavior across key screens, and made a series of responsive layout, hierarchy, legibility and markup cleanups.
